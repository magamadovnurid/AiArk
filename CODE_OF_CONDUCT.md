# Code of Conduct

AiArk contributors and maintainers are expected to keep project spaces respectful, constructive, and safe.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code without attacking people.
- Welcome participants regardless of experience, identity, background, or location.
- Give specific, actionable feedback and accept corrections in good faith.
- Respect privacy and never publish credentials, disk identities, private URLs, or vulnerability details.
- Put the safety of users and their data ahead of convenience or speed.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disclosure of private information, and sustained disruptive behavior are not accepted.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock discussions, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary. Reports will be reviewed as privately and fairly as practical.

This policy applies in the repository, issue tracker, pull requests, releases, and other project-managed spaces.
